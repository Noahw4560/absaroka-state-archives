## Communication
The OOC group chat is used for scheduling and coordination; IC legislative forums are the official venue for bills, hearings and impeachment.

## Legislation
The manual requires a **three-fourths majority of active senators** for passage, followed by the Governor's signature. A four-member active Senate ordinarily needs **three affirmative votes**.

## Hearings and impeachment
Any senator may request an in-state hearing and hearings may call witnesses and issue subpoenas. A hearing has no criminal sentencing power.

Impeachment articles require three-fourths approval. The initiating senator prosecutes and recuses from the conviction vote. Two senators plus the Chief Justice decide conviction with a two-of-three threshold; penalties are removal and disqualification.

## Procedural uncertainties
A seven-day hearing passage clause is not reconciled with the vote requirement. The 30-day-plus-first-of-next-month effective rule conflicts with a reference to June/December effective dates. Tie-breaking and tribunal membership selection also require clarification from server leadership.
