## Reported status
A March 23, 1922 *Absaroka Times* article says Senate Joint Resolution 74 had been signed into law during **autumn 1921** under **Public Resolution No. 25**, associated with the **Nuevo Paraíso Territorial Acquisition Act**.

## What is not documented
The precise text of the measure, boundaries, vote date, senator-level votes, and legal effect are not available. **Do not claim Henry Caldwell voted for or against it.** His appointment was on October 8, 1921 and the exact vote date is not supplied.
