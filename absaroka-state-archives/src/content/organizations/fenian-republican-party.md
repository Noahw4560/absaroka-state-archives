## Positions
The Fenian Republican Party promotes equality before the law, protection of civil liberties, worker rights, anti-corruption themes and Irish republican traditions. Boswell has also rejected communism.

## Political confrontation
Its public feud with ACP figures centers on national loyalty, ancestry, the Minutemen and disputed accusations of intimidation. A Blackthorn Battalion is associated with the movement. Accusations remain allegations unless corroborated.
