## Advertisement
The FOP's membership material identifies **Steven Candie** as founder and president and advertises legal defense, medical assistance, family support, employment advocacy, training, political access and a **$2 monthly membership fee**.

## Public dispute
The Zapatista League alleged that the FOP protected wealthy interests and enabled abuses of authority. The supplied poster does not identify a verified misconduct case; its claims should remain attributed to the League.
