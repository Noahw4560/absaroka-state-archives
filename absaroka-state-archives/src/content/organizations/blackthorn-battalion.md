## Identity
The Blackthorn Battalion appears in Fenian political materials and a poster attacking the ACP and Minutemen. The poster accuses ACP members of harassment, division and physical intimidation.

These are partisan claims rather than verified findings. The Battalion's membership, command structure and legal status have not been supplied.
