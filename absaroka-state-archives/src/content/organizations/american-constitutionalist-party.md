## Political themes
The ACP publicly emphasizes national loyalty, Christianity in civic identity, anti-communism, cultural assimilation, expansive gun rights, and criticism of revolutionary politics.

## Economic program
Hackney's October 30, 1922 signed statement advocates a living wage established by law, a Family Wage, pension proposals and ending company-store debts, while condemning private unions and syndicalism. This combination should not be reduced to an unqualified “anti-worker” position.

## Claims and controversies
The ACP's political opponents have alleged intimidation by Minutemen. The evidence supplied to this archive consists of opposing statements and propaganda, not proven convictions.
