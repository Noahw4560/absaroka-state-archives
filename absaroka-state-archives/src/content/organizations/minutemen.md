## Public record
ACP-affiliated broadcasts invite citizens to join the Minutemen. In his October 30, 1922 labor statement, Hackney instructed Minutemen to observe mining and railroad facilities, avoid unlawful force, and report suspected plots to marshals.

## Allegations
Fenian political materials allege intimidation and assaults by Minutemen. Those accusations remain **unverified** by independent records supplied here. Their actual charter and command structure have not been established.
