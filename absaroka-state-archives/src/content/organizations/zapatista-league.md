## Mission
The League promotes agricultural reform, workers' solidarity, anti-capitalist themes and opposition to American expansion into Mexico. It uses the motto **Tierra, Trabajo y Libertad**.

## Public conflicts
The organization has published critiques of Senator Whitcombe, Senator Hackney, and the Lemoyne FOP. Some publications advocate peace talks while others warn of forceful resistance to territorial aggression.

## Timeline and evidence
Its founding date is uncertain, with materials indicating activity in the 1921–1922 period. The League has made claims about territorial sovereignty, but the Mexican government's formal recognition or support is not established.
