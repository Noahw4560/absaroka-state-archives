## Record
Signed by Senator Cornelius Wiliford Hackney, District I, published by the ACP and listing John Henry Eden as editor.

## Policy positions
The statement claims support for a statutory living wage, a *Family Wage*, pensions under a *National Common Trust*, ending company-store debts, and the *People's Limit*. It strongly criticizes private union power and portrays revolutionary syndicalism as a threat to existing institutions.

It tells Minutemen to observe mine and railway infrastructure, remain within the law, and report suspected threats to marshals.

The statement makes unspecific claims about revolutionary activity without naming individuals or independent evidence. These claims should not be restated as established crimes.
