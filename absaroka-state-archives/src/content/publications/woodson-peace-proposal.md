## The appeal
Private citizen Oak Woodson affirmed that New Austin was under American sovereignty but argued that this carried obligations to protect Mexican residents as well as American travelers.

He proposed a gathering of local officials, merchants, ranchers, workers and Mexican community representatives to work on safe travel, water, roads, food, trade and the peaceful resolution of grievances.

## Later response
Emiliano Vargas publicly welcomed this proposal in a League poster. No completed meeting or agreement is established.
