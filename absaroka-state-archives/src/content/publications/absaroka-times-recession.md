## Newspaper summary
The **Absaroka Times**, vol. 26 no. 130, reported a severe postwar financial crisis tied to declining grain and cattle demand and restrictions on credit.

It reported that Governor Paul Cooper and Chief of Staff Richard Night supported drastic financial measures involving state auditors and troubled financial institutions. The newspaper questioned the effect on households and property values.

The story establishes the publication's account, not the precise legality or proven success of every financial measure. At publication, Henry Caldwell was still Mayor of Rhodes.
