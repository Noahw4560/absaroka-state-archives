## Newspaper summary
The **Absaroka Times**, vol. 26 no. 131, reported that the State Patrol acquired a fleet of motorcars delivered in Saint Denis. It identifies Lieutenant Governor Scott Adams and Colonel Rhett Madden as officials announcing the development.

Supporters said the cars would improve road law enforcement; critics warned that vehicles and road maintenance would burden taxpayers during a recession.
