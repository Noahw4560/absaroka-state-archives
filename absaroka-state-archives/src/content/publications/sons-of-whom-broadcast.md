## Announced program
A supplied broadcast announcement dated **1922** advertises John Henry Eden with an unnamed guest addressing a Fenian claim about patriotic Americans' immigrant parents.

Themes include Catholic missionaries, Indigenous history, Spanish conquest, cultural assimilation, and whether Americans who fight in Ireland can claim undivided national loyalty.

## Attribution
The announcement supplies a quote from “Our Guest,” but the guest's name is explicitly withheld. Although the player later identified these broadcasts as **Hackney-associated**, this does not prove Hackney was the guest or personally spoke every word.
