## Public address
John Bertelli, founder and CEO of **Bertelli Company Limited**, dated a public letter **March 14, 1922**. He described plans to expand employment for skilled and unskilled workers, increase manufacturing and construction activity, support training, and improve infrastructure.

He announced plans to cooperate with Mayor Theodore Beaumont and Saint Denis government. The letter says the company was established in 1898.

## Evidence boundary
This is an announcement of intended work and cooperation; individual completed projects have not been independently documented.
