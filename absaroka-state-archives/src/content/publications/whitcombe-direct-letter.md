## Letter summary
In a direct October 1922 letter, Whitcombe told Vargas that District IV had inherited years of neglect. He specifically criticized his predecessor Senator Bentley, although the archive has not independently verified the claims against Bentley.

Whitcombe said Mexican families should be able to settle peacefully in New Austin, recognized Vargas's right to speak and assemble, and warned him to avoid escalating political grievances into armed conflict.

## Interpretation caution
The warning may be interpreted by opponents as political pressure; Whitcombe expressly says it is not a threat. The source documents the exchange, not either side's hidden motives.
