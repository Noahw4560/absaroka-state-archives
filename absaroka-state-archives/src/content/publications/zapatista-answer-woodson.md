## To Oak Woodson
The Zapatista League welcomes Woodson's proposed meeting with local officials, merchants, ranchers, workers and Mexican representatives, and states that peace is preferable to bloodshed.

## To Senator Whitcombe
The League challenges Whitcombe to prove his New Austin development program through practical economic improvements rather than promises.

## Its declared stand
The poster reiterates land, fair wages, self-determination and Mexican-heritage communities' right to organize. It expresses readiness to negotiate in good faith without relinquishing its principles.
