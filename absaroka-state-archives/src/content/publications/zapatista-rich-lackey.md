## Allegations in the publication
The poster calls Whitcombe a servant of wealthy interests and accuses him of using fear and prejudice to divide Mexican and American workers. It appeals for solidarity among workers, peasants and families.

## Evidence note
These are **the League's political allegations**; the image does not provide independent proof of corruption, racial discrimination, or hidden industrial sponsors. The headline uses the original poster's spelling “Lacky.”
