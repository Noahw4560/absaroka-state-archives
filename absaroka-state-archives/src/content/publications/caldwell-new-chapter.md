## Publication context
The player-supplied poster uses Caldwell's office letterhead and addresses farmers, merchants, workers, lawmen, and entrepreneurs throughout the state.

It presents the coming period as an opportunity for communities to write the next chapter of Absaroka's story. It is aspirational rather than a policy announcement.

## Textual note
The image includes the unusual phrase “opportunity awaits for those who can’t,” likely a drafting error. The archive preserves the original image rather than silently correcting it.
