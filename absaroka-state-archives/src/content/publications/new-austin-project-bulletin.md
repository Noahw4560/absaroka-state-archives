## Newspaper report
The **New Austin Bulletin**, volume 8, number 105, dated **June 19, 1922**, announced Whitcombe's New Austin Project. It described planned investments in employment, healthcare, housing, policing, roads, schools and aid to farmers and ranchers, beginning in Armadillo and Tumbleweed.

The same edition contained an opinionated report on Emiliano Vargas, calling his movement radical while stating he was not then viewed as a direct threat to New Austin's people.

## Status
The paper documents an **announced plan** and its public presentation, not proof that every initiative was legally enacted or completed.
