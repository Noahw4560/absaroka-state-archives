## Stated argument
The League disputes Whitcombe's account of the controversy and argues that economic exploitation is the central issue. It calls for solidarity across Mexican and American communities and accuses Whitcombe of making the movement's heritage a loyalty test.

## Evidence note
The poster's accusation of “racialist” politics is an attributed claim, not a finding. It can be read alongside Whitcombe's original letter and public statement in the related documents.
