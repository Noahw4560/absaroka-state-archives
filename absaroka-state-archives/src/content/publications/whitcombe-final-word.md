## Public message
Whitcombe says the dispute is about lawful government and the security of New Austin, not heritage. He rejects political intimidation and armed confrontation and defends a proposed program of employment, healthcare, housing and law enforcement.

He also claims the **State Senate stands united**. Without a supplied joint vote or statement, this should be treated as his representation, not proof of a recorded unanimous Senate position.
