## Newspaper summary
The **Absaroka Times**, vol. 26 no. 135, dated March 23, 1922, reports that **Senate Joint Resolution 74** was signed into law in autumn 1921 under **Public Resolution No. 25**. It describes American territorial demands directed toward Mexico and a claimed mistranslation of the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo.

Mexican authorities are depicted as pursuing negotiations, while rumors of military exercises circulate.

## Evidence boundary
The article does not provide the full legal text, Caldwell's roll-call vote, a confirmed declaration of war, or proof that all the reported diplomatic claims are legally sound.
