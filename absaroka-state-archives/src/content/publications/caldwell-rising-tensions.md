## Publication context
This is an original image of a Caldwell office statement supplied by his player. It acknowledges disagreements among citizens, organizations and elected representatives, including New Austin and organized labor.

The statement emphasizes that freedom of expression should be accompanied by respect for the rights and dignity of others. It presents peace as a willingness to listen rather than universal agreement.

## Provenance
Original player-provided poster; this record describes what the poster says and does not claim any legislation or vote followed it. The heading in the original image reads **“On the Rising Tensions of Absaroka.”**
