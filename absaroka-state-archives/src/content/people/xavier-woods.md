## Public policy
Woods supports worker safety, fair wages, and lawful labor organizations while also emphasizing economic stability and avoiding coercion. He publicly defended **Revenue Bill 1-1921-03**, a proposal associated with an additional six dollars in hourly wage standards.

The proposal's exact terms and final voting status are unavailable. Similar concerns for working people do not establish a formal alliance with Senator Caldwell.
