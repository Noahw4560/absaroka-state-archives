## Municipal policy
Beaumont promotes education, civic improvement, business development, and a form of lawful worker advocacy. In June 1922 he welcomed the sale of Saint Denis's last horse stable to a motorcar company while insisting that traditional trades and transportation remained important.

A March 14, 1922 public address by John Bertelli announced an intention to cooperate with Beaumont and the city government on employment and industrial expansion. This indicates **an announced collaboration**, not proof every promised project was completed.
