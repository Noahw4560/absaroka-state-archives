## Office
Guthrie McKinley succeeded Henry Caldwell as mayor of Rhodes. Caldwell's final day in office was October 8, 1921; McKinley was documented acting as mayor by October 11, 1921.

## Platform
McKinley emphasizes business development, roads, transportation modernization, honesty, and orderly union advocacy. A previously circulated hostile poster naming a “Governor McKinley” may refer to a different timeline or person; it should not be attributed to this mayor without verification.
