## Executive history
Earlier documents place Paul J. Cooper as lieutenant governor under Peter Beckwith, acting governor from around February 13, 1921 following Beckwith's incapacitation. The September 29, 1921 *Absaroka Times* calls him **Governor Paul Cooper** and connects his office to emergency financial measures.

## Unresolved identity
A **November 1, 1921 roster instead names James Cooper as Governor**. The material does not establish whether Paul J. Cooper and James Cooper are the same person, successive governors, or a continuity error. This archive retains separate entries.
