## Public broadcasts
Eden is identified as a radio broadcaster for **The Torchbearer**, whose programming is paid for or published by the American Constitutionalist Party. Broadcasts address national loyalty, Christian nationalism, anti-communism, the Fenians, and Senator Hackney.

An advertised special titled *Sons of Whom?* featured an anonymous guest. The source provided a program description, but the anonymous guest's identity remains unverified.

The October 30, 1922 Hackney labor statement also lists Eden as editor, describing him as Minister of National Enlightenment and Minuteman Brigade-Leader. These are titles within the in-character publication.
