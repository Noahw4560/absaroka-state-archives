## Roles and positions
Killmore has been described as National Guard Captain and Assistant Adjutant General. He was previously a Saint Denis mayoral candidate and withdrew citing military obligations. His public rhetoric emphasizes policing, lower taxes, economic growth, anti-communism and immigration assimilation.

## Unverified controversy
In John Bertelli's Morello assassination account an attacker allegedly invoked Killmore and the ACP. That does not establish Killmore's involvement, and Killmore denied responsibility.
