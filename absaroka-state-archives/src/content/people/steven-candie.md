## FOP founding
Steven Candie was identified as founder and president of the Lemoyne County Fraternal Order of Police, formed in 1921. The advertisement also identifies him as a special agent with Annesburg Private Police.

The organization advertised officer legal representation, medical and family relief, job protections, training and political advocacy. The Zapatista League later attacked the FOP in its propaganda. No specific FOP crimes have been independently established by the supplied posters.

**Do not conflate Steven Candie with Attorney General Calvin Candie.** Their relationship, if any, is unknown.
