## Public priorities
Scott Adams emphasizes government accountability, the rule of law, public safety, farmers, small businesses, workers and teachers.

The October 6, 1921 *Absaroka Times* reports that Adams and Colonel Rhett Madden announced the State Patrol's first automobile fleet. Adams framed it as a public-safety modernization; the newspaper notes criticism of maintenance and taxpayer costs.

Under the supplied Senate manual the lieutenant governor casts tie-breaking votes, but how that interacts with a three-fourths threshold remains unclear.
