## Business announcement
On **March 14, 1922**, Bertelli issued a public address stating that Bertelli Company Limited intended to expand employment, construction, manufacturing, infrastructure and training in Saint Denis in collaboration with Mayor Theodore Beaumont. His letter identifies the company as established in 1898.

## Morello controversy
A separate February 12, 1921 letter described Bertelli witnessing the shooting of businessman John Morello. He reported an attacker invoking other political figures, but this allegation does not establish those figures' involvement.
