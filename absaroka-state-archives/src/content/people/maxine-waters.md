## Election and office
The October 13, 1921 *Absaroka Times* reports that Maxine Waters took office as Mayor of Blackwater on **October 7, 1921**, after Martino Giordano resigned. The newspaper describes her as Blackwater's first Black mayor.

Her earlier government experience included public safety administration and communications. Public priorities include equal opportunity, community participation, impartial policing, and local business.
