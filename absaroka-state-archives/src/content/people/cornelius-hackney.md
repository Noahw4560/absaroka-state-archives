## Political identity
Cornelius Wiliford Hackney, commonly titled **Commodore**, appeared as District I Senator in the November 1, 1921 government roster. His associated party is the **American Constitutionalist Party (ACP)**.

Associated political rhetoric emphasizes national loyalty, a Christian cultural conception of America, anti-communism, strong private firearm rights, and territorial expansion. His signed October 30, 1922 statement criticized private unions and syndicalism while endorsing a statutory living wage, family wage and pension proposals.

## Political controversies
The Torchbearer broadcast said charges against Hackney had been dropped; the basis for their dismissal is unknown. Opponents have alleged misconduct involving Minutemen, but those allegations have not been proven in the supplied records. A broadcast featuring an anonymous guest is associated with his party; the guest's personal identity is unresolved.
