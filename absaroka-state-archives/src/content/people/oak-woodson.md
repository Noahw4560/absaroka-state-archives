## Status
The player confirmed that **Oak Woodson is a private citizen**. His appeal does not make him a senator or authorize him to bind the state in negotiations.

## “A Shared Future, Built in Peace”
Woodson recognized New Austin as American territory while urging equal protection for Mexican and American residents. He proposed a gathering of local officials, Mexican community representatives, merchants, ranchers and workers to discuss safe travel, trade, food supplies, and grievances.

The Zapatista League publicly **welcomed** his proposed meeting. There is no confirmed record of a meeting actually occurring.
