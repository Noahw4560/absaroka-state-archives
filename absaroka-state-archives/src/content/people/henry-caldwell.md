## Established biography
Henry James Caldwell was born in Rhodes in 1877 to German immigrant parents. He enlisted in the U.S. Army in 1893 at the age of sixteen, served during the Spanish–American War, and has a player-established final rank of **Major**. He later worked as a farmer and carpenter.

Caldwell served as **Mayor of Rhodes from March 27 to October 8, 1921**. He left the mayoralty to accept an appointment as **District II Senator on October 8, 1921**, a date explicitly confirmed by his player.

## Political outlook
Caldwell is an Independent constitutional progressive. He supports accountability, civil liberties, agriculture, private enterprise, reasonable wages and voluntary unions. He opposes coercion and worries about the economic cost of unsustainable labor demands.
