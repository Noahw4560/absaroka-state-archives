## Organization
Boswell's Fenian political movement emphasizes Irish republican heritage, civil liberties, equal rights and workers' rights. He has also said that communists are political enemies of his movement.

## Contested claims
Boswell stated that he formerly participated in the ACP Minutemen and witnessed intimidation of opponents. Those claims are contested and are **not independently established** by the supplied materials. He has been publicly criticized by the ACP for fighting in Irish political struggles.
