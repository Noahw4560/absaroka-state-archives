## District IV
Henry Whitcombe is documented as a New Austin senator. He favors private enterprise, development, direct employer-employee bargaining, and opposition to coercive union practices.

## New Austin Project
A June 19, 1922 **New Austin Bulletin** promoted his plan for employment, healthcare, housing, roads, schools, agricultural assistance and law enforcement. The announcement is evidence of proposals, not proof of their completion.

## Dispute with Emiliano Vargas
Whitcombe condemned threats of armed conflict and published a direct letter to Vargas in October 1922. He acknowledged Vargas's freedom to speak and assemble while warning against revolutionary confrontation. He criticized his predecessor **Senator Bentley** for neglecting the district; those are Whitcombe's allegations.

The Zapatista League replied with attacks on Whitcombe's economic interests and alleged treatment of Mexican communities. Those are partisan claims, not established verdicts.
