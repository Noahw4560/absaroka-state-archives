## Event
The September 29, 1921 *Absaroka Times* describes economic distress across the state and emergency intervention under Governor Paul Cooper and Chief of Staff Richard Night.

The newspaper does not prove the policy's ultimate success or provide a complete legal audit. Caldwell was still Mayor of Rhodes at the time.
