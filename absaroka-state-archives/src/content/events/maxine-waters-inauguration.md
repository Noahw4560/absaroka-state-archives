## Event
The October 13, 1921 *Absaroka Times* reports Waters took her oath on **October 7, 1921**, after the resignation of former mayor Martino Giordano.

The newspaper subsequently reported Giordano's arrest in Annesburg; unverified organized-crime rumors should not be treated as convictions.
