## Snapshot
The **November 1, 1921** roster names Governor James Cooper; Lieutenant Governor Scott Adams; Attorney General Calvin Candie; Chief Justice Jayden Wesley; Chief Judge Daniel Morrell; and senators Cornelius Hackney, Henry Caldwell, Xavier Woods and Henry Whitcombe.

It also lists several sheriffs and mayors. This record is a snapshot, not evidence the same officials served at all dates in 1922 or 1923.
