## Event
The October 6, 1921 *Absaroka Times* reports delivery of a State Patrol motorcar fleet in Saint Denis after delays associated with the recession.

Officials framed the vehicles as a way to pursue road crime, while critics pointed to purchase, fuel and maintenance costs.
