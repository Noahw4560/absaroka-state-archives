## Appointment
The player confirmed **October 8, 1921** as both Caldwell's final day as Mayor of Rhodes and the date of his appointment as District II Senator.

The November 1 roster confirms he was serving at that later date, but **November 1 was not the appointment date**. The appointing authority and vacancy circumstances are unknown.
