## Political correspondence
Whitcombe's October 1922 letter acknowledges Vargas's free expression and criticizes the prospect of armed confrontation. Subsequent League materials accuse Whitcombe of favoritism toward wealthy interests and ethnic division.

The materials show a public controversy, not proven criminal findings against either figure.
