## Initiative
In a 1922 Saint Denis Gazette appeal, Oak Woodson proposed a meeting involving local officials, merchants, ranchers, workers and Mexican community representatives.

The Zapatista League welcomed the proposal. A completed meeting or binding negotiated agreement has **not been established**.
