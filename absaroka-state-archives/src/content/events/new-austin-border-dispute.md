## Conflict
The *Absaroka Times* reported in March 1922 that **Resolution 74** had been enacted in autumn 1921, that the U.S. demanded territory around Nuevo Paraíso, and that Mexican authorities were delaying or negotiating the demand.

The League made separate claims concerning **New Austin**. These are distinct geographic and political assertions. No full treaty, legal text, roll call or confirmed war declaration has been supplied.
