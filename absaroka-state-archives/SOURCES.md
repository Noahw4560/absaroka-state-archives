Other content seed records are **descriptive summaries of previously supplied user materials** including the Senate Manual, dated office roster, newspapers, and other letters. Some have no accompanying scan in this initial repository. They are explicitly marked and do not contain new invented court judgments, votes or office transitions.

**Not included:** Full original copies of every prior radio recording; complete texts of the Beckwith Act, Resolution 74 and other measures; all historical Discord messages. Add these when their sources can be verified and you are authorized to publish them.
